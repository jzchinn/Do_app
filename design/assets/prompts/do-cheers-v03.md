Preserve the balanced chunky glass shapes and inward tilt. Make symbol occupy 65% of canvas width, centered, with comfortable padding. Professional vector-like logo, perfectly smooth crisp edges. No text. 1024×1024 PNG.

Tool: built-in image_gen; model and seed not exposed.
